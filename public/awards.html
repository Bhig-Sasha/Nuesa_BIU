About the Awards

Where Excellence Meets Recognition

Recognising excellence, leadership, creativity and meaningful contributions within NUESA.

The NUESA BIU Awards is an annual celebration of the brightest minds, most dedicated leaders, and most inspiring contributors in the Faculty of Engineering at Benson Idahosa University.

From the freshest freshers to seasoned student lecturers, we recognise those who go above and beyond to make our community stronger, smarter, and more connected.
